About Me

For me, data isn’t just numbers on a screen, it’s the closest thing we have to a record of human behavior, decisions, and systems. I get a real kick out of digging into messy datasets and pulling out insights that make people pause and say, “I hadn’t thought of it that way before :o".

I have worked on projects that span predictive modeling, natural language processing, and deep learning. Along the way I have become fluent in Python, SQL, and R, and I specialize in transforming messy datasets into clear insights that drive decisions. I enjoy the balance of statistical rigor with practical application, moving easily between theory and implementation.

What excites me most is the transformation of raw data into models, stories, and tools that create real impact. Whether it is analyzing long-term trends, building AI systems that recognize emotion, or designing analytics that guide business strategy, I thrive on work that is both technically sharp and meaningful.
