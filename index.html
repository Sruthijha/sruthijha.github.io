About Me

"It's simple, do it better than anyone else."

Data is only as powerful as the questions you ask of it. I'm drawn to the hard ones, where there are no obvious answers, and the real value lies in finding something that actually changes how decisions are made.

Nobel Prize-winning economist Ronald Coase once said, "Torture the data, and it will confess to anything." That idea precisely shapes how I approach real-world data, questioning assumptions, being careful with patterns, and focusing on insights that actually hold up.

I recently graduated from the University of Colorado Boulder with a Master's in Data Science. Coming from an electrical engineering background, the shift was a natural one: both fields focus on understanding complex systems, just at different levels of abstraction. I'm currently a Data Intern at Goodie Bag, where I support customer acquisition and marketplace growth through data analysis. I'm especially interested in work where analysis connects directly to real outcomes, not just dashboards, but decisions that drive impact.

Outside of work, I'm naturally curious and enjoy learning new things, whether it's a technical concept or an unfamiliar idea. I grew up watching Naruto, and that mindset, persistence and long-term thinking, is what brought me from an engineering student in Hyderabad to building data systems in the US, and what continues to drive me forward.
