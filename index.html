About Me

I see data as more than just numbers on a screen - it's one of the closest things we have to a record of how people think, decide, and build systems. I enjoy studying complex problems, working with messy data, and turning ambiguity into insights that genuinely influence real decisions.

I recently graduated from the University of Colorado Boulder with a Master's in Data Science, and I'm currently working as a Data Intern at Goodie Bag, where I support data-driven decisions around customer acquisition and marketplace growth. I'm most excited by work that blends thoughtful analysis with real-world impact - where data is used not just to describe what happened, but to help shape what comes next.
