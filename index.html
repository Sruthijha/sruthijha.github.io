About

I got into data science because it feels like investigating. Finding the thing nobody noticed and turning it into something that changes a decision. That's the part I love.

I'm from Hyderabad. Studied electrical engineering and found my way into data through curiosity more than planning. I did my master's at CU Boulder, and along the way worked at a tractor plant, an insurance company, a food startup, and now Pfizer. Each role pushed me into a different kind of problem, from physical systems to messy operations to large-scale unstructured data.

I paint sometimes. I read fiction. I play above-average chess. I like problems where the answer isn't immediately obvious, and I tend to stick with things longer than I probably should.
