Data Intern

Pragmatic Insurance Broking Services · Remote
Operations Analyst Intern
Jun – Aug 2024

At Pragmatic, a small insurance brokerage, the operational backbone ran on manual spreadsheets, renewal trackers, quotation sheets, weekly MIS reports, all maintained by hand. I structured that data and streamlined the reporting, cutting the time it took to produce the weekly reports and easing the manual comparison brokers did every day. Watching them work taught me what structured data actually enables when you get it right.

Intech Insurance Surveyors · Hyderabad
SDE Intern → Data Scientist
Aug 2022 – Jun 2023

Insurance fraud isn't obvious. That's the problem. Intech's investigators were buried, hundreds of claims, no way to know which ones needed attention first. I helped build a prioritization layer that scored and surfaced high-risk claims from a pool of 5,000+ records. Supervisors stopped guessing and started triaging. The system didn't catch fraud on its own. It made sure the right people looked at the right claims first.

Mahindra & Mahindra · Zaheerabad
Project Intern
Jun – Jul 2022

My first job was inside Asia's largest tractor plant. 181 tractors a day, exports to 65 countries, and me, a third year EEE student trying to figure out why machines kept breaking down. I researched the root causes, tested solutions, and found the fix. It cost Rs 5,450. It saved the plant Rs 60 lakh every time a machine failed. Somewhere in Zaheerabad, that machine is still running.
